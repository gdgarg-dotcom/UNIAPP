# POSITIONING_SYSTEM

## Purpose

The Positioning System is the strategic decision guide for UniApp.

Its purpose is to determine the strongest professional positioning for a target role by selecting the most relevant narrative, prioritizing the most relevant capabilities, and improving recruiter alignment.

The Positioning System defines **how the candidate should be positioned**. All factual evidence is retrieved from the Career Knowledge Base.

---

## Responsibilities

- Select the Primary Positioning Narrative.
- Select one Supporting Narrative where appropriate.
- Prioritize capabilities based on role relevance.
- Guide recruiter-first positioning.

# Positioning Principles

## One Professional Identity

Present one clear professional identity.

Avoid combining competing narratives.

---

## Primary + Supporting Narrative

Select one Primary Narrative.

Select one Supporting Narrative only if it strengthens the Primary Narrative.

---

## Relevance Over Completeness

Prioritize information that directly supports the target role.

Exclude information that weakens the positioning.

---

## Evidence Over Assumption

Support every positioning decision with evidence from the Career Knowledge Base.

Never introduce unsupported claims.

---

## Positioning Changes Emphasis, Not Facts

Change emphasis only.

Never change chronology, responsibilities, ownership, achievements, metrics, or technologies.

---

## Recruiter First

Optimize first for recruiter understanding and role alignment.

Use ATS optimization to support recruiter readability.

---

## Consistency

Apply the same positioning logic to similar job descriptions.

Maintain a consistent professional brand across resumes, LinkedIn, cover letters, and other career assets.

## Software vs. Hardware Positioning

Note: Software is the default and only positioning built into current resumes. Hardware-specific evidence (e.g. the Redington B2B marketplace deal, which involves routers/switches/wireless products) is deliberately excluded from resume narrative detail, even though the underlying partnership and revenue figure may still be cited generically. A hardware-positioned variant is not maintained as a stored resume — candidate can adapt manually if ever needed.

---

## India Positioning

### Lead With

- India-specific scope and achievements
- APJC as supporting regional context (not a separate market list)

### Avoid Leading With

- Explicit EMEA / Americas market mentions in narrative text (Career Summary, role bullets) — these are factual but deliberately not surfaced in India-targeted resumes per the relevance gate
- Broad global market lists in the Career Summary

Note: Role-scope facts that are quantified and India-inclusive (e.g. "14+ APJC markets") may remain, since APJC includes India and the figure is evidence, not a narrative market list.

---

## GCC / UAE Positioning

### Lead With

- GCC mentioned once in the Career Summary (for ATS relevance)
- "Open to Relocation – GCC" / "Open to Remote Opportunities – GCC" on its own line, above contact details
- Role-scope language reframed as "GCC and global markets" in place of full region lists

### Avoid Leading With

- India-specific-only framing
- The same detailed APJC/EMEA/Americas market list used in factual leadership records — simplify to GCC + global

## Guiding Principle

The Positioning System exists to answer one question:

**What is the strongest professional positioning for this role?**

If a piece of information does not help answer that question, it belongs in another Knowledge Base file.
