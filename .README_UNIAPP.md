\# UniApp



Structured strategic resume architecture for benchmark-aligned resume generation, optimization, auditing, and retrieval.



\---



\## Architecture



01\_Knowledge\_Base → Reusable strategic evidence and positioning assets



02\_Benchmarks → Canonical role benchmark definitions



03\_Masters → Benchmark-aligned master resumes



04\_Variants → Strategically differentiated resume variants



05\_Project\_Configs → System prompts, workflows, configs, and operational instructions



\---



\## System Flow



Knowledge Base → Benchmarks → Masters → JD Alignment → Variants



\---



\## Design Principles



\* Low entropy architecture

\* Benchmark purity

\* Strategic reuse

\* Minimal duplication

\* Retrieval-friendly structure

\* LLM-optimized organization

\* Semantic consistency

\* Master-first orchestration



\---



\## Folder Purpose



\### 01\_Knowledge\_Base



Reusable:



\* achievements

\* executive summaries

\* positioning narratives

\* keywords

\* projects

\* tools

\* writing standards



Maintained as:



\* reusable strategic evidence infrastructure

\* benchmark-agnostic intelligence layer

\* upstream support layer for benchmark-aligned Masters



\---



\### 02\_Benchmarks



Defines:



\* target role ecosystems

\* strategic positioning

\* benchmark expectations

\* role boundaries



Acts as:



\* benchmark calibration layer

\* role-definition framework

\* alignment system for Master Resumes



\---



\### 03\_Masters



Canonical benchmark-aligned resumes built from:



\* benchmarks

\* selectively incorporated strategic evidence



Used as:



\* canonical strategic assembly layer

\* primary source for benchmark-aligned variant generation

\* reusable retrieval and optimization layer



Designed for:



\* benchmark purity

\* strategic consistency

\* reusable positioning

\* low redundancy

\* controlled optimization



\---



\### 04\_Variants



Strategically differentiated resume variants aligned to benchmark ecosystems.



Variants are generated through benchmark-aligned Master Resumes combined with contextual JD alignment and controlled optimization workflows.



Only preserve:



\* meaningful strategic differentiation

\* reusable positioning value

\* low-overlap variants

\* variants that cannot be efficiently regenerated



\---



\### 05\_Project\_Configs



Operational system layer containing:



\* prompts

\* workflows

\* audit logic

\* generation instructions

\* system architecture references

\* orchestration rules

\* hidden optimization workflows



\---



\## Operational Rules



\* Maintain strict benchmark alignment.

\* Use benchmark-aligned Masters as the primary source for variant generation.

\* Avoid direct raw aggregation during resume generation.

\* Avoid duplicate variants.

\* Remove weak or regenerable resume variants.

\* Preserve only strategically reusable assets.

\* Do not create unnecessary folders or abstraction layers.

\* Keep architecture semantically stable and low entropy.

\* Preserve benchmark purity and strategic role clarity during optimization.
