# WRITING_STYLE_GUIDE

Use this style when generating all resume outputs.

## TONE
- executive
- concise
- business-first
- credible
- metrics-driven
- strategic

## PREFERRED STYLE
- short, direct bullets
- clear action verb + outcome
- concrete business language
- natural keyword usage
- differentiated achievements
- strong hierarchy and scanability

## AVOID
- fluffy adjectives
- buzzword overload
- generic corporate phrasing
- repeated sentence openings
- long paragraphs
- robotic language
- exaggerated claims
- keyword stuffing

## GOOD BULLET SHAPE
Action -> what changed -> business outcome

Example:
- Improved pipeline conversion by 20% through Sales-Marketing alignment and targeted nurture optimization.

## GOOD SUMMARY SHAPE
Role fit + specialization + years of experience + business impact.

Example:
Growth marketing leader with 20 years of experience driving enterprise demand generation, GTM operations, and AI-enabled marketing transformation across global technology organizations.

## NORMALIZATION RULES
- Use full forms on first mention when helpful.
- Avoid unnecessary abbreviations unless they are industry standard.
- Keep section names stable.
- Keep content modular and easy to retrieve.
