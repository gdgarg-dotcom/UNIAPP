WRITING\_STYLE\_GUIDE



Use this style when generating all resume outputs.



TONE



executive

concise

business-first

credible

metrics-driven

strategic



PREFERRED STYLE



short, direct bullets

clear action verb + outcome

concrete business language

natural keyword usage

differentiated achievements

strong hierarchy and scanability

strategic compression over excessive explanation

modular and reusable phrasing



AVOID



fluffy adjectives

buzzword overload

generic corporate phrasing

repeated sentence openings

long paragraphs

robotic language

exaggerated claims

keyword stuffing

benchmark contamination

unnecessary duplication



GOOD BULLET SHAPE



Action -> what changed -> business outcome



Example:



Improved pipeline conversion by 20% through Sales-Marketing alignment and targeted nurture optimization.



GOOD SUMMARY SHAPE



Role fit + specialization + years of experience + business impact.



Example:



Growth marketing leader with 20 years of experience driving enterprise demand generation, GTM operations, and AI-enabled marketing transformation across global technology organizations.



NORMALIZATION RULES



Use full forms on first mention when helpful.

Avoid unnecessary abbreviations unless they are industry standard.

Keep section names stable.

Keep content modular and easy to retrieve.

Preserve semantic consistency across benchmark, master, and variant layers.

Prioritize reusable strategic language over highly contextual phrasing.



MASTER \& VARIANT GENERATION RULES



Resume variants should primarily derive from benchmark-aligned master resumes.



Masters act as the canonical strategic assembly layer because they already contain:



\* benchmark-calibrated positioning

\* prioritized evidence selection

\* role-aligned narrative structure

\* optimized strategic emphasis



Knowledge Base assets should support master refinement and evolution through reusable:



\* achievements

\* projects

\* metrics

\* positioning signals

\* tools

\* strategic capabilities



Use Knowledge Base assets selectively to:



\* enrich masters

\* add new strategic evidence

\* strengthen benchmark alignment

\* improve business impact specificity

\* expand reusable proof points



Avoid constructing variants directly from raw Knowledge Base aggregation unless intentionally rebuilding or evolving a benchmark-aligned master.
