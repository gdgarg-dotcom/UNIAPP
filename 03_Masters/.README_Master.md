\# Master Resumes



Purpose:

Canonical benchmark-aligned master resumes for each strategic role ecosystem.



Each Master Resume:



\* maps directly to one Benchmark

\* selectively incorporates reusable evidence from the Knowledge Base

\* acts as the canonical strategic assembly layer for resume variants

\* preserves benchmark-aligned positioning and narrative structure



Masters are designed for:



\* maximum reusable strategic coverage

\* low redundancy

\* benchmark purity

\* semantic stability

\* strategic consistency

\* variant generation



Variants should primarily derive from benchmark-aligned Master Resumes, with Job Descriptions acting as contextual optimization signals for role alignment, terminology calibration, and evidence prioritization.



Rules:



\* One Master Resume per Benchmark.

\* Preserve strong reusable evidence.

\* Avoid employer-specific tailoring.

\* Avoid JD-specific optimization.

\* Maintain semantic alignment with Benchmark definitions.

\* Preserve benchmark purity and strategic role clarity.

\* Avoid direct Knowledge Base aggregation during variant generation unless intentionally evolving the Master itself.



Architecture Flow:

Knowledge Base → Benchmarks → Masters → Variants
