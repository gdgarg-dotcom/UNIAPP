# Head of ABM — Benchmark Master JD

## Role Purpose
Lead account-based marketing strategy for enterprise growth, with ownership of target account engagement, sales alignment, and pipeline influence. This benchmark reflects a senior leader driving 1:1, 1:few, and 1:many ABM programs.

## Scope of Responsibility
- Define ABM strategy by account tier, segment, and buying committee.
- Build account selection, scoring, and prioritization models with Sales and RevOps.
- Orchestrate personalized, multi-channel account journeys.
- Use intent data and engagement signals to improve account penetration.
- Partner with Sales to support opportunity creation, acceleration, and expansion.
- Develop executive reporting for account engagement and pipeline contribution.
- Align content, campaigns, and enablement assets to account-specific priorities.

## Core Competencies
- ABM strategy
- Target account orchestration
- Intent data and predictive intelligence
- Sales enablement
- Pipeline influence
- Account engagement
- Buying committee marketing
- Cross-functional alignment

## ATS Keywords
ABM, account-based marketing, target accounts, account engagement, intent data, Demandbase, 6sense, Bombora, buying committee, account penetration, 1:1 ABM, 1:few ABM, 1:many ABM, sales alignment, account orchestration, pipeline influence, revenue marketing, enterprise marketing, personalization, account scoring.

## Success Signals
- Increased engagement in strategic accounts
- Improved pipeline contribution from target account programs
- Effective orchestration across sales and marketing
- Strong use of intent and account intelligence
- Clear differentiation by account tier

## Benchmark Purity Notes
Emphasize account strategy, sales collaboration, and account engagement above broad demand generation or paid media execution.
