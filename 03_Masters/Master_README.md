\# Master Resumes



Purpose:

Canonical benchmark-aligned master resumes for each strategic role ecosystem.



Each Master Resume:



\* maps directly to one Benchmark

\* pulls evidence from Knowledge Base

\* acts as the primary retrieval source for future resume generation



Masters are designed for:



\* maximum reusable strategic coverage

\* low redundancy

\* benchmark purity

\* variant generation



Rules:



\* One Master Resume per Benchmark.

\* Preserve strong reusable evidence.

\* Avoid employer-specific tailoring.

\* Avoid JD-specific optimization.

\* Maintain semantic alignment with Benchmark definitions.



Architecture Flow:

Knowledge Base → Benchmarks → Masters → Variants
