# POSITIONING_GUARDRAILS

These rules prevent over-positioning and conflicting narratives.

## PRIMARY RULE
Each resume must have:
- one primary narrative
- one secondary supporting narrative

Do not combine all archetypes equally.

## DO NOT
- over-emphasize AI transformation unless the JD requires it
- over-emphasize paid media unless the JD requires it
- mix unrelated narratives into one resume
- create an "everything expert" profile
- inflate scope, seniority, or ownership
- repeat the same metric across multiple sections unless necessary

## DO
- choose the strongest archetype for the JD
- use supporting archetypes only when they strengthen the story
- preserve a believable career progression
- keep the executive profile consistent across sections
- maintain a single business narrative
- prioritize the most relevant proof points first

## NARRATIVE DISCIPLINE
The final resume should read as if the candidate has a coherent executive specialty, not a stitched-together collection of unrelated strengths.
