# VP Demand Generation — Benchmark Master JD

## Role Purpose
Own the enterprise demand engine across the full funnel, with accountability for pipeline creation, revenue influence, and sales alignment. This benchmark represents a senior B2B demand generation leader in multi-channel, multi-stakeholder environments.

## Scope of Responsibility
- Lead integrated demand generation strategy across paid, owned, earned, and partner channels.
- Drive pipeline creation, acceleration, and conversion across the funnel.
- Partner with Sales, RevOps, Product Marketing, and regional business teams.
- Build and optimize ABM programs for strategic accounts and account clusters.
- Manage budget allocation, forecasting, and performance governance.
- Establish attribution, reporting, and executive visibility for demand performance.
- Improve lead quality, MQL-to-SQL conversion, and pipeline contribution.

## Core Competencies
- Full-funnel demand generation
- ABM strategy and execution
- Pipeline acceleration
- Sales-marketing alignment
- Revenue attribution
- Lead lifecycle optimization
- Multi-channel campaign orchestration
- Executive reporting and governance

## ATS Keywords
Demand generation, pipeline, revenue, ABM, account-based marketing, lead generation, lead nurturing, lead scoring, MQL, SQL, conversion, attribution, intent data, demand engine, pipeline acceleration, revenue marketing, sales alignment, B2B marketing, enterprise marketing, campaign orchestration, funnel optimization.

## Success Signals
- Consistent pipeline growth quarter over quarter
- Improved lead-to-opportunity conversion
- Measurable ABM penetration into target accounts
- Strong forecast discipline and budget efficiency
- Clear sales partnership and revenue impact

## Benchmark Purity Notes
Emphasize pipeline, revenue, ABM, and funnel performance more than brand, pure media buying, or generic marketing operations.
