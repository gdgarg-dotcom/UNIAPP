# Paid Media Director — Benchmark Master JD

## Role Purpose
Lead paid media strategy and performance governance across search, social, video, programmatic, and other acquisition channels. This benchmark represents a senior media leader accountable for budget efficiency, scale, and return on investment.

## Scope of Responsibility
- Own paid media strategy, planning, and optimization across major platforms.
- Manage campaign performance, budget allocation, and pacing.
- Improve ROAS, CPA, CPC, CTR, and conversion efficiency.
- Partner with Analytics, Creative, and Demand Gen teams.
- Build audience strategies, retargeting plans, and media experiments.
- Provide performance reporting and executive visibility.
- Align media investment to business goals and funnel priorities.

## Core Competencies
- Paid media strategy
- Media buying and optimization
- Performance governance
- Audience segmentation
- Budget management
- Attribution and reporting
- Cross-channel acquisition
- Testing and experimentation

## ATS Keywords
Paid media, media buying, digital advertising, Google Ads, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, DV360, Meta Ads, YouTube Ads, programmatic, ROAS, CPA, CPC, CTR, optimization, budget management, audience segmentation, retargeting, performance marketing, attribution, funnel efficiency.

## Success Signals
- Efficient budget utilization
- Strong return on ad spend
- Improved acquisition and conversion metrics
- Clear testing discipline
- Reliable reporting and optimization cadence

## Benchmark Purity Notes
Emphasize media strategy, optimization, and ROI more than brand campaigns or broad marketing leadership.
