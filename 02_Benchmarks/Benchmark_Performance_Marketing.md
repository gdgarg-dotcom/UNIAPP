# Head of Performance Marketing — Benchmark Master JD

## Role Purpose
Own performance marketing across acquisition channels with accountability for revenue impact, CAC optimization, and scalable growth. This benchmark represents a senior leader who blends media strategy, experimentation, and analytics.

## Scope of Responsibility
- Lead performance strategy across paid search, paid social, programmatic, and related channels.
- Optimize CAC, CPA, ROAS, and conversion performance.
- Manage budget allocation, forecasting, and pacing.
- Run testing programs across creative, audience, landing pages, and bidding.
- Partner with Growth, Analytics, Creative, and Revenue teams.
- Ensure attribution and reporting accuracy.
- Translate channel performance into business impact.

## Core Competencies
- Performance marketing strategy
- Acquisition optimization
- Budget allocation
- Conversion improvement
- Attribution
- Experimentation
- Audience strategy
- Revenue impact

## ATS Keywords
Performance marketing, CAC, CPA, ROAS, acquisition, paid search, paid social, programmatic, conversion rate, attribution, experimentation, budget management, optimization, landing page testing, audience strategy, funnel performance, digital growth, ROI.

## Success Signals
- Lower acquisition costs
- Higher conversion and return on spend
- Strong testing discipline
- Reliable reporting and decision support
- Clear connection to revenue growth

## Benchmark Purity Notes
Emphasize performance, optimization, and measurable returns more than brand marketing or broad marketing leadership.
