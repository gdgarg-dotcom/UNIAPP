\# Benchmarks



Purpose:

Canonical role-definition layer for UniAppSys.



Each benchmark defines:



\* target role positioning

\* strategic narrative

\* hiring-market alignment

\* benchmark expectations

\* keyword direction

\* role boundaries



Benchmarks act as:



\* role archetypes

\* strategic reference layers

\* alignment anchors for Master Resumes

\* calibration frameworks for benchmark-aligned positioning



Benchmarks primarily guide the creation, calibration, and evolution of benchmark-aligned Master Resumes.



Rules:



\* One benchmark per strategic role ecosystem.

\* Maintain clear benchmark separation.

\* Avoid overlap and role contamination.

\* Do not store tailored variants here.

\* Preserve benchmark purity and role-specific strategic clarity.



Architecture Flow:

Knowledge Base → Benchmarks → Masters → Variants
