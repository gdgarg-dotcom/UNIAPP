# VP Growth Marketing — Benchmark Master JD

## Role Purpose
Own growth strategy across acquisition, conversion, and retention motions, with accountability for measurable business outcomes. This benchmark represents a senior growth leader who combines experimentation, channel strategy, analytics, and performance optimization.

## Scope of Responsibility
- Define growth strategy across the full customer lifecycle.
- Improve acquisition efficiency, conversion rates, and revenue contribution.
- Lead experimentation across landing pages, campaigns, messaging, and journeys.
- Use attribution and funnel analytics to prioritize growth investments.
- Partner with Product, Sales, Marketing, and Analytics teams.
- Build scalable growth loops and repeatable acquisition systems.
- Manage budget allocation and ROI optimization across channels.

## Core Competencies
- Growth strategy
- Acquisition optimization
- Conversion rate improvement
- Lifecycle marketing
- Experimentation and testing
- Attribution and analytics
- Funnel optimization
- Cross-functional execution

## ATS Keywords
Growth marketing, acquisition, conversion, experimentation, A/B testing, funnel optimization, lifecycle marketing, retention, CAC, LTV, attribution, revenue growth, product marketing alignment, digital growth, analytics, ROI, growth loops, scaling, optimization.

## Success Signals
- Improved CAC and conversion performance
- Strong experimentation cadence
- Scalable growth systems
- Measurable business impact across funnel stages
- Data-led investment decisions

## Benchmark Purity Notes
Prioritize growth outcomes, conversion, and analytics over pure brand management or only media execution.
