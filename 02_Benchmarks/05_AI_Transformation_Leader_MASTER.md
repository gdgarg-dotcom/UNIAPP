# AI Transformation Leader — Benchmark Master JD

## Role Purpose
Lead AI-enabled transformation across marketing, operations, and customer engagement systems. This benchmark represents a senior leader who translates AI strategy into business outcomes, process improvements, and scalable operating models.

## Scope of Responsibility
- Define AI strategy and transformation roadmap for marketing or growth functions.
- Identify high-value AI use cases and operational opportunities.
- Build or oversee AI-enabled workflows, automation, and copilots.
- Improve productivity, speed-to-market, and decision quality.
- Collaborate with leadership, operations, product, and technology teams.
- Govern implementation, adoption, and change management.
- Communicate business value, risk, and rollout plans to executives.

## Core Competencies
- AI strategy
- Transformation leadership
- Workflow automation
- Operating model design
- Change management
- Use-case prioritization
- Cross-functional adoption
- Executive advisory

## ATS Keywords
AI transformation, GenAI, automation, workflow automation, operating model, transformation roadmap, AI strategy, use cases, copilots, productivity, change management, adoption, orchestration, LLM, prompt engineering, automation governance, digital transformation, future-state design.

## Success Signals
- Clear AI strategy linked to business outcomes
- Practical implementation, not just conceptual vision
- Improved productivity or efficiency
- Successful change management and adoption
- Executive trust and stakeholder alignment

## Benchmark Purity Notes
Emphasize transformation, AI use cases, and operating models more than generic marketing delivery or paid media execution.
