# Head of Digital Marketing — Benchmark Master JD

## Role Purpose
Lead digital marketing across web, content, paid, owned, and conversion channels with responsibility for traffic, engagement, demand capture, and performance outcomes. This benchmark reflects a senior digital leader managing integrated digital ecosystems.

## Scope of Responsibility
- Own digital strategy across web, content, paid, email, and conversion journeys.
- Increase traffic, engagement, conversion, and demand capture.
- Partner with brand, growth, performance, product marketing, and analytics teams.
- Improve website experience, landing pages, and digital journeys.
- Govern reporting, attribution, and digital KPI dashboards.
- Manage digital agencies and cross-functional stakeholders.
- Ensure digital programs support business goals.

## Core Competencies
- Digital strategy
- Web and funnel optimization
- Content and campaign integration
- Conversion optimization
- Reporting and analytics
- Cross-channel coordination
- Digital governance
- Stakeholder management

## ATS Keywords
Digital marketing, website optimization, conversion, content marketing, paid media, email marketing, web traffic, SEO, SEM, digital campaigns, analytics, attribution, funnel optimization, digital performance, landing pages, journey optimization, dashboards, omni-channel, engagement.

## Success Signals
- Growth in traffic and engagement
- Strong conversion performance
- Clear digital governance and reporting
- Cross-functional coordination
- Business-aligned digital execution

## Benchmark Purity Notes
Emphasize digital ecosystem leadership and conversion impact more than offline brand or pure field marketing execution.
