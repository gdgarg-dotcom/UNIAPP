# Head of Brand Marketing — Benchmark Master JD

## Role Purpose
Lead brand strategy, messaging, and market positioning to strengthen awareness, differentiation, and preference. This benchmark represents a senior brand leader who connects brand building with commercial impact.

## Scope of Responsibility
- Define brand positioning, narrative, and messaging architecture.
- Strengthen awareness, recall, differentiation, and consideration.
- Partner with product, growth, content, and executive leadership.
- Lead brand campaigns, storytelling, and integrated communications.
- Manage brand guidelines, governance, and consistency.
- Measure brand performance through research, perception, and engagement.
- Translate brand strategy into market and commercial impact.

## Core Competencies
- Brand strategy
- Positioning and messaging
- Storytelling
- Integrated communications
- Brand governance
- Market perception
- Campaign leadership
- Executive alignment

## ATS Keywords
Brand marketing, brand strategy, positioning, messaging, storytelling, awareness, consideration, recall, brand equity, integrated communications, brand governance, narrative, differentiation, thought leadership, creative strategy, market perception, campaign strategy, brand lift.

## Success Signals
- Clear brand differentiation
- Measurable lift in awareness or recall
- Strong narrative consistency
- Executive alignment on messaging
- Effective integrated brand campaigns

## Benchmark Purity Notes
Emphasize brand positioning, narrative, and perception more than performance media or demand generation execution.
