# Head of Field Marketing — Benchmark Master JD

## Role Purpose
Lead regional and event-driven field marketing programs that connect demand generation with sales execution and local market activation. This benchmark represents a senior field marketing leader driving pipeline through region-specific campaigns and in-market engagement.

## Scope of Responsibility
- Own field marketing strategy for regions, segments, or business units.
- Plan and execute local campaigns, events, roadshows, and executive programs.
- Align field teams with sales goals, target accounts, and pipeline priorities.
- Work with ABM, demand generation, product marketing, and sales teams.
- Measure event, campaign, and regional pipeline impact.
- Manage budgets, agencies, and execution timelines.
- Ensure localization of global campaigns for market relevance.

## Core Competencies
- Field marketing
- Regional activation
- Event strategy
- Sales alignment
- Local pipeline generation
- ABM support
- Campaign execution
- Stakeholder coordination

## ATS Keywords
Field marketing, regional marketing, events, roadshows, local activation, sales alignment, pipeline, regional demand generation, account engagement, localization, campaign execution, event ROI, in-market activation, territory support, pipeline contribution, regional programs.

## Success Signals
- Strong local pipeline contribution
- Effective sales collaboration
- Successful event and activation programs
- Localization of global strategy
- Clear measurement of in-market impact

## Benchmark Purity Notes
Emphasize local activation, events, and sales support more than national brand strategy or media optimization.
